The parties irrevocably accept, declare, and undertake that, together with all other provisions of the said GCA, this agreement shall also be valid and binding for them as is. 1. Definitions: In addition to the definitions set forth in the GCA, the following definitions shall be used as stated below in the interpretation and implementation of this agreement. 1.1. Bank: Kuveyt Türk Katılım Bankası A.Ş. 1.2. Inflation Calculation Formula: (loan amount * Number of Maturity Days * the average of the monthly inflation differences to be taken retrospectively for a period of one year starting from the monthly inflation rate announced on or before the installment date at the closest date) / 30 1.3. Actual expense: Any monetary amount requested and collected from the Customer in return for the amounts spent directly or indirectly by the Bank under personnel expenses, system expenses, operational expenses or under other names in order to carry out the transactions subject to this agreement. 1.4. Public obligations: Amounts paid directly to public institutions such as taxes, duties, fees, funds, revolving funds. 1.5. Customer: Natural or legal persons who constitute a party to this agreement and whose name/title and signature appear at the end of the agreement. 2. Taksitler, anapara, enflasyon (Enflasyon Hesaplama Formülü ile bulunacak) farkı, kamusal yükümlülükler ve fiili masraflarla birlikte tahsil edilir. 3. Genel Hükümler 3.1. İşbu yöntem ile kullandırılan krediler dolayısıyla, Müşterinin erken kapama hakkı bulunmakta olup, mevzuat ve/veya GKS'de düzenlenen erken kapama koşullarına riayetle erken kapama yapabilecektir. 3.2. Katılım bankacılığı esasları uyarınca enflasyon farkı uygulamasında sadece değer kaybının telafisi amaçlanmakta olup, herhangi bir getiri amacı yoktur. 3.3. Geri ödeme vadesi (taksit tarihleri) tarafların ittifakıyla belirlenmiş kesin vade olduğundan, vadenin tahakkukuyla borç muaccel hale gelir. Borcun vadesinde ödenmediği hallerde başkaca bir işleme lüzum olmaksızın temerrüt oluşur. 3.4. Taraflar arasındaki ilişkiye 11.01.2011 tarih ve 6098 sayılı Türk Borçlar Kanunu'nun Tüketim Ödüncü başlığı altındaki hükümleri uygulanmaz. 2.2. If the information in the Customer's request matches the limit/collateral conditions, the Bank may decide to disburse the credit and disburse the requested loan amount by depositing it into the Customer's account. 2.3. Repayment of the loan: Due to the loan to be used by the Customer, the Bank shall request from the Customer, at the time of repayment, the amount determined by adding the amount calculated by the Inflation Calculation Formula to the loan amount, as well as public obligations and actual expenses. Accordingly: 2.3.1. A repayment (installment) plan is created on the disbursement date for the loan granted. 2.3.2. Installments are collected together with principal, inflation difference (to be calculated with the Inflation Calculation Formula), public obligations, and actual expenses. 3. General Provisions 3.1. For credits disbursed under this method, the Customer has the right of early repayment and may perform early repayment in compliance with the early repayment conditions stipulated in the legislation and/or the GCA. 3.2. In accordance with the principles of participation banking, in the application of the inflation difference, only compensation for loss of value is intended, and there is no intention of generating any return. 3.3. Since the repayment maturity (installment dates) has been determined as a definite maturity by mutual agreement of the parties, the debt becomes due upon the occurrence of the maturity. In cases where the debt is not paid on its due date, default shall occur without the need for any further action. 3.4. The provisions under the title of Consumption Loan in the Turkish Code of Obligations No. 6098 dated 11.01.2011 shall not apply to the relationship between the parties. Banka/Bank Müşteri/Customer
