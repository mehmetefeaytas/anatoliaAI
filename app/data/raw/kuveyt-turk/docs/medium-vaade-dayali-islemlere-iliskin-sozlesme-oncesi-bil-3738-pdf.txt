Bu anlamda taraflar arasında bir çıkar çatışması sözkonusu olmayacaktır. / As a rule, in forward transactions, our Bank does not take a position against the Customer. Therefore, the Customer's loss due to exchange rate fluctuations does not mean that our Bank will make a profit to the same extent. In other words, the Customer's loss does not result in the Bank's profit. In this regard, there will be no conflict of interest between the parties. In cases of conflict of interest, the Bank acts in accordance with its policies and practices on this matter.
