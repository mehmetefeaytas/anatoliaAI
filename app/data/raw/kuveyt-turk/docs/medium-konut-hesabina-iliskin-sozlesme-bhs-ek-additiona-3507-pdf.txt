Minimum and maximum limits for the regular monthly payment schedule are TRY 4.511,50 and TRY 45.115,04 respectively. If payments are made as to the quarterly payment schedule, three times of the montly maximum and minimum limits are valid.. These amounts are increased each year up to the revaluation rate determined and declared in accordance with the Tax Procedure Law No.213 and dated 4/1/1961 relating to the previous year.
