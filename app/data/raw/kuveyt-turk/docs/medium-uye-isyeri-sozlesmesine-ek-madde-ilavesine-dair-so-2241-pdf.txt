II) Subject The bank provides funds to its customers through electronic banking applications in accordance with the interest -free banking principles and standards and the legislation it is subject to, and also offers its customers the opportunity to make payments through the same channels. And the Member Workplace offer products and services to their customers and allow them to shop. This Additional Agreement, was concluded for the purpose of regulating the mutual rights and liabilities of the parties regarding providing Bank funds to customers who will purchase goods or services from the Member Workplace and acting as the Bank's agent in these transactions within the scope of this Additional Agreement and payment of the prices of goods/services sold by the Member Workplace directly from the customer accounts without the use of funds and money transfer transactions to be carried out in the accounts of the Member Workplace at the Bank. IV) Special Provisions regarding Financing and Money Transfer Transactions via CebimPOS: 1. Member Workplace transmits the necessary information to the Bank through the CebimPOS application to sell products and services to Customers visiting the Member Workplace. The Member Workplace will generate a QR code according to this information transmitted by the Bank and the Member Workplace in accordance with the Regulation on the Production and Use of TR QR Code in Payment Services and the system, technical documents, principles and rules, guides and other legislative provisions regarding QR Code Production and Use. The QR Code generated by the Bank is sent to the Member Workplace via the CebimPOS application. The Customer scans the QR Code offered to them by the Member Workplace through their Mobile Device and the money transfer or financing request process is completed depending on the transaction to be performed. Transaction cancellations are also made via the QR Code created by the Bank based on the data sent through the Member Workplace. 2. By signing this Additional Agreement, the Bank does not undertake in any way to provide Financing to all customers shopping at the Workplace or to a certain number of customers; each Customer's Financing request will be evaluated freely by the Bank in accordance with the Bank's own internal regulations and rules.
